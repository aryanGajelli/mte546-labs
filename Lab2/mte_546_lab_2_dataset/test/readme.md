Description of variables

data: thermocouples in columns 1:4, IR sensors in columns 5:8
time: timestamps of each measurement
sample_rate: Sampling Frequency in Hertz
headers: Names and positions of the sensors aligned with the columns of data

Exact positions are measured from the origin, the furthest corner of the plate from the distance sensors, to the center of the block.
The filename contains the xy-coordinates in mm
